# Cascade

Cascade is a quiet game of Klondike on a deep green felt table. Seven columns begin with just one face showing in each, and the stock waits above them. Uncover the hidden cards as you build each foundation from Ace to King.

Drag a card or an alternating color run to make room below it. An empty column welcomes a King. The stock turns three cards at a time, and the waste shows the latest turn in a small fan. When the stock runs out, turn it again to begin another pass.

A double click sends a playable card home when its foundation is ready. Finish all four suits and the table erupts: cards launch, bounce, and leave their shapes across the felt before the final victory message appears.
